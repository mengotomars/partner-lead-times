Average days per phase

What it shows: the typical number of days each stage takes, across the briefs shown above (finished stages only). How to read it: the stacked bar at the top adds the stage averages together into one typical brief, from Briefing Start to launch, for the period chosen in the start-date filter (last 90 days by default). Below it, each stage on its own: longer bar = slower stage. Colours match the teams in the timeline. Late send to partner is the days Partnerships sent after the 1st of the brief month, counting on-time sends as 0. Use it to: see where the process loses the most time on average.

Median days per phase

What it shows: the middle value for each stage: half the briefs were faster, half were slower (finished stages only, briefs shown above). How to read it: compare it with the average above. If the average is much higher than the median, a few very slow briefs are dragging the average up, and a typical brief is closer to the median. Use it to: tell a stage that is slow for everyone apart from one that has a handful of outliers.
